About

Hello! I am Jiawei Tong, a 3rd-year Ph.D. candidate in Information Systems at the University of Melbourne (expected completion: September 2027).

My research examines AI accountability, explainable AI (XAI), responsible AI governance, and human–AI teaming, adopting a behavioural Information Systems lens to understand how people evaluate, rely on, and attribute responsibility to AI systems in organizational contexts.
